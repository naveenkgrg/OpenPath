# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

OpenPath is a community resource. Everyone who contributes or participates is expected to behave respectfully — toward other contributors, toward the communities we reference, and toward beginners who are just starting out.

Specifically:
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing experience levels and perspectives.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Dismissing or mocking beginners or people learning.
- Spam, self-promotion without community value, or promotional behavior disguised as contribution.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all spaces managed by OpenPath — pull requests, issues, and any community discussions.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue or contacting the maintainers. Maintainers will review reports and take appropriate action, which may include removing content or banning contributors.
