## Safety and Due Diligence

Before applying or participating:

- Verify the program or organization through **official websites and public community references**.
- Avoid opportunities that request **payment, fees, or personal financial information**.
- Look for **clear program details**, timelines, selection criteria, and contact information.
- Confirm communications originate from **official domains or well-known public channels**.

---

## Important Notes on Participation

- Always review and follow the **project’s terms and conditions**, contribution guidelines, and code of conduct.
- Internship and job eligibility may depend on **local laws, visa status, and work authorization rules**.
- Contributors are responsible for ensuring they are **legally permitted to participate** in any paid or unpaid opportunity.
- OpenPath does not verify eligibility, sponsorship, or compliance requirements.
