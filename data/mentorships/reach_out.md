# How to Reach Out

Guidance for contacting maintainers or mentors respectfully.

- Read the project contribution guide and Code of Conduct first.
- Use the project's preferred channel (issue tracker, Slack, Discord, mailing list).
- Be concise: mention your background, the issue you want to work on, and your plan.
- Ask clear questions and show you tried to find answers.
- Follow up politely if you do not hear back.

If a project has a mentorship program, prefer its official application or onboarding path.
